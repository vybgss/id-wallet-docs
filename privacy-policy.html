Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding any information you provide while using our iOS application, ID Wallet. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information. By using ID Wallet, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

ID Wallet does not require you to create an account or provide any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your card data (including titles, QR code and barcode contents, code formats, expiration dates, locations, colors, and icons) is stored in your own iCloud account and synced to your devices that are signed in to the same Apple Account. We do not have access to it.

Some information never leaves the device where it is created: when you last opened each card (used to sort cards by recent use and to choose the cards shown in widgets), the Apple Wallet exports you have purchased but not used yet (export credits), and your app settings. This information is not synced to your other devices and is never sent to us.

When you choose to export a card to Apple Wallet, your card data (title, code content and format, expiration date, location coordinates, color, and a random identifier the app creates for the card) is transmitted to our backend server solely for the purpose of generating and digitally signing the Apple Wallet pass. This is required by Apple's PassKit framework to create valid wallet passes. The request does not include your name, your Apple Account, or any device identifier.

2. Usage Data

ID Wallet does not contain analytics, advertising, or tracking code and does not collect usage data itself. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics for the app. This data helps us improve app reliability and does not include personally identifiable information.

3. Permissions

ID Wallet requests the following optional permissions to enable specific features:

  • Camera Access: Used only for scanning QR codes and barcodes when you choose the "Scan" option. Codes are recognized on your device; no photos or videos are stored or transmitted.
  • Photos: When you choose to import a code from a photo, you select the photo in Apple's photo picker. The app receives only the photo you select, reads the code from it on your device, and does not keep the photo. It does not access the rest of your photo library.
  • Location Services (When In Use): Used only when you choose to add a location to a card for lock screen relevance, to show your current position on the map. The place you choose (its name and coordinates) is saved with the card and synced through iCloud like the rest of the card. Its coordinates are included only in passes you export to Apple Wallet. The app does not track your location in the background.
  • Notifications: Used for expiry reminders three days before and on the day a card expires. Reminders are scheduled on your device and show the card's title; no notification server is involved. You can turn them off in the app's settings.

You can use the app without granting these permissions. Denying permissions will only limit the specific features that require them (e.g., you can still create cards by typing text manually instead of scanning).

4. How We Use Your Information

When you export a card to Apple Wallet, the card data transmitted to our backend server is used exclusively for:

  • Generating the Apple Wallet pass file with your card information
  • Digitally signing the pass using Apple's required cryptographic certificates
  • Returning the signed pass file to your device for installation in Apple Wallet

We do not store, retain, or use your card data for any other purpose. Pass data is processed in real-time and is not permanently stored on our servers after the signing process is complete.

5. Third-Party Services

ID Wallet does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The only communication with our own server occurs when you explicitly choose to export a card to Apple Wallet, at which point your card data is sent to our backend server for pass signing as described above.

The app relies on the following Apple services, which handle your data according to Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • iCloud: stores your cards and syncs them between your devices.
  • App Store: processes purchases of Apple Wallet exports. We do not receive your payment details; the app only learns that a purchase succeeded.
  • Apple Maps: shows maps and finds places when you add a location to a card, and shows the map on a card's screen. Place searches and map locations are sent to Apple for this purpose.
  • Apple Wallet: stores the passes you add to it.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, and Spotlight: card titles are made available to iOS so you can open a card by name with Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, or the Action button.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your cards are stored with Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your Apple Account and are synced to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account that have ID Wallet installed. iCloud data is protected by Apple's security measures, and we cannot access it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your cards are kept on your device. Recent-usage data, export credits, and settings are stored only on your device and are protected by Apple's standard security and sandboxing measures.

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show a copy of your cards that the app saves on your device, in a storage area shared only between the app and its widgets. Widgets do not connect to the internet or to iCloud. Codes shown in widgets are hidden while your device is locked, and Lock Screen widgets show only a card's icon and title.

Cards you delete in the app are removed from iCloud. Because cards sync, a card that is still on another of your devices can return from there, so delete it on each device where it appears. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device, but your cards stay in your iCloud account and return if you reinstall the app; to remove them, delete your cards in the app first. Unused export credits are lost when you delete the app.

When you export a card to Apple Wallet, the card data is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend server for pass signing. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect data during transmission and processing. Once the signed pass is returned to your device, it is handed off to Apple's Wallet app, which manages the pass according to Apple's privacy policies and security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

ID Wallet is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal identifying information from anyone, including children under 13. Card data is only transmitted to our backend server when the user explicitly chooses to export a card to Apple Wallet, and only for the purpose of pass signing as described above.
