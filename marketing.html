Features

Everything a card needs

Simple to set up, quick to use, and built entirely around your iPhone.

  • Scan in seconds

    Point the camera at a code, pick a screenshot from Photos, or type the number. QR codes and common barcodes — EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39, PDF417, Aztec and more — are recognized.

  • Add to Apple Wallet

    Turn any card into a Wallet pass. It appears on your Lock Screen near the place you set and goes with you on Apple Watch.

  • Ready at checkout

    Tap a code to show it full screen at maximum brightness. The screen stays awake, and barcodes can be rotated for a bigger scan target.

  • Make it yours

    Eight gradient colors and 23 icons — or let ID Wallet pick an icon from the card's name. Add an expiry date and a place.

  • Widgets

    Keep your favorite or most recent cards on the Home Screen and Lock Screen. Codes stay hidden while your iPhone is locked.

  • Siri & Shortcuts

    Say “Show City Gym in ID Wallet” to open a code hands-free, or put it on the Action button.

  • Expiry reminders

    Get a notification three days before a card expires and on the day itself, so a membership never runs out by surprise.

  • iCloud sync

    Cards sync automatically across your iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. Nothing to set up.

  • Light & Dark

    Looks great in Light and Dark Mode, with accent colors to match your style.

Apple Wallet

Pay once per pass

Adding a card to Apple Wallet is a one-time purchase for that pass, with the price shown in the app in your currency. Everything else is included, and there are no subscriptions.

  1. Open a cardand tap Add to Apple Wallet.
  2. Confirm the purchasewith the App Store, just like any in-app purchase.
  3. Tap Addto save the pass. If you don't finish adding it, the purchase is kept for your next try.

Private by design

Your cards are yours. ID Wallet works without an account and keeps your data with you and Apple.

  • No account, sign-in, or personal details needed
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking
  • Cards are stored in your own iCloud — we can't see them
  • Our server sees a card only to sign a pass you add to Wallet, and doesn't keep it
