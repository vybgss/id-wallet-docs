Getting Started

How do I add a card?
  1. Tap + on the home screen (or Scan a Code if your wallet is empty).
  2. Choose Scan Code, Choose from Photos, or Enter Manually.
  3. Give the card a name. For codes that contain a web address, ID Wallet suggests one.
  4. Optionally pick a color and icon, set an expiry date, or add a location.
  5. Tap Add. The card opens, ready to show or add to Apple Wallet.
Which codes are supported?

QR codes and common barcodes: EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, ITF-14, PDF417 and Aztec. When you type a number, ID Wallet picks the barcode type for you (13 digits — EAN-13, 8 digits — EAN-8, anything else — Code 128), and you can change it in the editor.

Can I add a card from a screenshot?

Yes. Choose Choose from Photos and pick the screenshot or photo with the code. ID Wallet reads the code on your iPhone. It only receives the photo you pick, not your library, and doesn't keep the photo.

How do I show a code at the checkout?

Open the card and tap its code. It opens full screen at maximum brightness, and the screen stays awake until you close it. Barcodes can be rotated for a bigger scan target.

Faster ways: long-press a card on the home screen and choose Show Code, ask Siri “Show City Gym in ID Wallet”, or tap the card in an ID Wallet widget.

How do I edit or delete a card?

Open the card and tap Edit to change its name, code, color, icon, expiry date or location. To delete it, tap ⋯ and choose Delete Card, or long-press the card on the home screen. Deleting a card doesn't remove a pass you added to Apple Wallet — remove that in the Wallet app.

Which devices does ID Wallet run on?

iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later. Cards you add to Apple Wallet also appear on your paired Apple Watch.

Apple Wallet

How do I add a card to Apple Wallet?
  1. Open the card and tap Add to Apple Wallet.
  2. Confirm the one-time purchase for this pass. The price is shown in the app.
  3. Check the pass preview and tap Add.

An internet connection is needed, because each pass is digitally signed as Apple requires.

What does a Wallet pass give me?

The pass keeps the card's name, code, color and expiry date. If the card has a location, Apple Wallet suggests the pass on your Lock Screen when you're nearby. Passes also appear on your paired Apple Watch.

I edited a card. Does the pass in Wallet update?

No. A pass is a copy of the card at the moment you added it. To get the new version into Wallet, add the card again (this is a new purchase) and remove the old pass in the Wallet app.

Why does my barcode look different in Wallet?

Apple Wallet supports QR, PDF417, Aztec and Code 128 codes. Other barcodes, such as EAN-13 or UPC, are drawn as Code 128 with the same number, in Wallet and in the app. Most checkout scanners read it; if one doesn't, the cashier can type the number shown under the code.

How do I remove a pass from Apple Wallet?

Open the Wallet app, tap the pass, tap ⋯ and choose Remove Pass. ID Wallet can't remove passes for you, and deleting a card in ID Wallet leaves its pass in Wallet.

Purchases

What do I pay for?

Only for adding a card to Apple Wallet: each pass is a one-time purchase. Everything else — cards, scanning, widgets, Siri, reminders and iCloud sync — is included. There are no subscriptions.

I paid but didn't finish adding the pass. Did I lose it?

No. A purchase is used only after the pass is actually saved in Wallet. If you cancel, lose the connection or close the app, it's kept for your next try, and ID Wallet won't ask you to pay again. Unused purchases are shown in Settings → Export Credits.

Can I restore purchases on a new iPhone?

Each purchase pays for one pass, so passes you already added stay in Apple Wallet and there's nothing to restore. An unused purchase stays on the iPhone where you made it: it doesn't move to other devices, and it's lost if you delete the app.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud & Your Data

Do my cards sync between devices?

Yes. Cards sync automatically through iCloud to your iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. Settings → iCloud Sync shows whether iCloud is available. If you're not signed in to iCloud, cards are kept on your iPhone only.

A card doesn't show up on my other iPhone.

Make sure both iPhones are signed in to the same Apple Account and are online, then open ID Wallet on each. Syncing can take a few minutes.

I deleted a card, but it came back.

The card was still on another of your iPhones and synced back from there. Delete it on each iPhone where it appears.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your cards stay in iCloud and come back when you reinstall ID Wallet. Unused purchases, recent-use order and settings are kept only on the iPhone, so they're lost. Passes in Apple Wallet aren't affected.

Who can see my cards?

Only you. There's no account, and cards are stored in your own iCloud. Card data reaches our server only when you add a card to Apple Wallet, to sign the pass, and isn't kept. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Widgets, Siri & Reminders

How do I add a widget?
  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen (or the Lock Screen and tap Customize), then tap Edit → Add Widget and choose ID Wallet.
  2. Pick Card for one card (Home Screen or Lock Screen) or Cards for up to six.
  3. To choose specific cards, touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget. Otherwise it shows the cards you used most recently.
Why is the code hidden in my widget?

Codes are hidden while your iPhone is locked, so nobody can use them from your Lock Screen or StandBy. Unlock your iPhone to see them. Lock Screen widgets show only a card's icon and name; tap one to open the code.

A widget shows an old version of a card.

Widgets show your cards as of the last time ID Wallet was open on that iPhone. If you changed a card on another device, open ID Wallet once and the widget catches up.

How do I use Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button?

Say “Show City Gym in ID Wallet” (with your card's name) to open its code full screen, or “Scan a card with ID Wallet” to start the scanner. Both actions are also in the Shortcuts app and in Spotlight. On iPhone models with the Action button, open Settings → Action Button, choose Shortcut, and pick an ID Wallet action.

How do expiry reminders work?

If a card has an expiry date, ID Wallet notifies you at 9:00 three days before it expires and on the day itself. Tap the notification to open the card. You can turn reminders off in Settings → Expiry Reminders.

Permissions

Why does ID Wallet ask for camera access?

Only to scan codes. Codes are recognized on your iPhone, and no photos or video are saved or sent. Without camera access you can still pick a photo or type the code.

Why does ID Wallet ask for my location?

Only to show where you are on the map when you add a place to a card. You can also search for the place instead. ID Wallet doesn't track your location in the background.

Why does ID Wallet ask to send notifications?

For expiry reminders. ID Wallet asks the first time you save a card with an expiry date.

I said no to a permission. How do I turn it on?

Open the iPhone Settings app, scroll down to Apps → ID Wallet (on older iOS versions, just ID Wallet), and turn on Camera, Location or Notifications.

Troubleshooting

The scanner doesn't recognize my code.

Fill the viewfinder with the code, hold your iPhone steady, and turn on the flashlight in low light. Glossy cards scan better at a slight angle. If it still doesn't work, choose Enter Manually and type the number printed under the barcode.

Adding to Apple Wallet fails.

Check your internet connection and try again. If you already paid, the purchase is kept and you won't be charged again. If the problem continues, write to us with the time it happened and the message you saw.

The checkout scanner can't read my code.

Open the code full screen so it's at maximum brightness, and hold the iPhone still a few centimeters from the scanner. Old laser scanners can't read screens at all — then the cashier can type the number shown under the code.
